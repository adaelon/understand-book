Understand Book uses the original v8 medium-touch FLAC recordings unchanged: 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo. No lossy encoding or sample-rate/bit-depth reduction.
The upstream instrument records in minor thirds and maps those recordings to adjacent keys.
The mapping preserves the upstream fast-response offsets and records the upstream retuned cents.
Per-key playback adjustments align the measured early sustained fundamental to A4=440 Hz equal temperament.
The reference window is 0.10–0.50 seconds after sample start; low notes below 100 Hz use 0.6–3.6 seconds. Natural string beating and decay remain.
Recordings are loaded by musical range. Other velocity layers, release-noise and pedal-noise recordings are available upstream.
