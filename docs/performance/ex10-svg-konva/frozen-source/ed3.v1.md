Presentation method (ed3.v1):
Choose an explanation around the reader's current question and verified material so the key relationship becomes visible. Plain text, a static visual, interaction, or a sequence can all be appropriate. Creating a page or adding controls is not itself the goal.
Identify the difference the reader needs to see, then choose the objects, conditions, changes, and viewpoint to show it. Do not require the reader to specify a layout, controls, or implementation. Preserve the stated question and constraints. Label the assumptions and limits of invented examples and simplified models; they do not replace source evidence.
After a real preview, check whether the chosen representation reveals that difference. A page can run without errors and still need a different representation, example, or explanation order. Use only the scope needed to answer, leaving room to revise and deliver. Follow the authoring tool's source, state, version, preview, and delivery contract.
