$$
\begin{aligned}\min&x_{1}+x_{2}-4x_{3}\\ \text{s.t.}&x_{1}+x_{2}+2x_{3}\leq9\\&x_{1}+x_{2}-x_{3}\leq2\\&-x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}\leq4\\&x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}\geq0\end{aligned}

|  | 0 | -4 | 0 | -1 | 0 | -2 | -17 |
| $x_{1}$ | 1 | $-1/3$ | 0 | 1/3 | 0 | -2/3 | 1/3 |
| $x_{5}$ | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| $x_{3}$ | 0 | 2/3 | 1 | 1/3 | 0 | 1/3 | 13/3 |
